Diseases we treat

Most common in fescue and ryegrass lawns during hot, humid stretches.

Dollar spot

Light tan or white dead patches. Often shows up in humid summer weather or when the lawn is low on nitrogen.

Leaf spot

Brown spots or a burned-out look. Tall fescue and ryegrass are prone, from late spring through summer.

Red thread

Red or pink blotches, most common in perennial ryegrass and fine fescue. Low nitrogen and poor drainage contribute.

Brown patch

Circular brown areas in the lawn.

Summer patch

Patches of dying grass during summer heat.

What causes lawn fungus?

Humidity, low nitrogen, mowing too short, poor drainage and the wrong watering schedule all raise the risk.

Which grasses are most at risk?

Tall fescue, ryegrass and fine fescue, especially in summer.

Is fungicide part of the Green Lawn Program?

Fungicide is a separate program. If you’re on our lawn program, your technician watches for fungus at every visit and tells you if treatment is needed.
