Concept · How it works

What sits behind the website.

A new website brings in more estimate requests. What happens after the form decides whether they become customers who renew. This page maps that path and the systems behind it.

Anything marked “to confirm” is an assumption for discussion.

From estimate request to renewing customer

Seven steps, one customer record the whole way through.

  1. 1Estimate formAddress first, then contact details
  2. 2CRM recordContact and deal created in HubSpot
  3. 3Branch routingZIP code assigns one of 7 branches
  4. 4Fast follow-upA rep calls back within minutes in business hours
  5. 5Program bookedCustomer and program created in the field service system
  6. 6Route and visitTechnician app, lawn flag, photo summary
  7. 7Portal and alertsTexts, visit history, approvals, billing

The systems map

The first thing to confirm is which field service platform runs routing and billing today. Everything else connects to it.

Customer-facing

  • Website on HubSpot CMS
  • My Lawn portal
  • Text and email alerts

Revenue

  • HubSpot CRM
  • Phone system and call tracking
  • Referral and prepay programs

Operations

  • Field service platform for scheduling, routing and billing (to confirm)
  • Technician mobile app
  • Fleet telematics for 200+ vehicles

Back office

  • Payments and accounting
  • HR, recruiting and seasonal onboarding
  • Licensing and product-use records

Data

  • One reporting layer across everything above
  • Branch and route dashboards
