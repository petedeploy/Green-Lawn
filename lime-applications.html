What is liming?

Applying a material such as limestone to reduce soil acidity and raise pH.

How does lime help my lawn?

In the right pH range, more nutrients are available to grass roots, so the lawn grows better.

Why is soil acidic in PA, NJ and DE?

Rainfall washes out buffering minerals, and acid rain and decaying organic matter add acidity.

What else does balanced pH do?

It improves nutrient availability, root growth, soil structure and soil microorganism activity.

How do I know if I need it?

We test your soil pH for free and recommend lime only if it will help.
