What are grubs?

Beetle larvae that feed on grass roots. They’re among the most widespread and destructive lawn pests in the Northeast.

When is grub control applied?

Late spring or early summer, before grubs can damage turf.

Is it included in the Green Lawn Program?

Yes. The program includes one grub control application each year.

Is it guaranteed?

Yes. Grub control applications are guaranteed for the year.
