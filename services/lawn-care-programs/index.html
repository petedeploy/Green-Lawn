The Green Lawn Program questions

Hiring a lawn company for the first time can feel like a gamble. Here are straight answers to what homeowners ask us first.

Get a free estimate

or call 888-581-5296

What does the program include?

Seven fertilizer treatments, one grub control application, unlimited weed control, free service calls and a thatch density analysis.

How often will you service my lawn?

Eight visits a year, about every 4 to 6 weeks, timed to the growing season.

Do I need to be home?

No. Your technician leaves a lawn flag and a service summary after every visit.

What if weeds show up between visits?

Call us. Service calls are free.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes, once the application dries. On a dry day that’s usually within a couple of hours.

What if it rains after a treatment?

Light rain helps the lawn absorb fertilizer. Heavy rain right after a weed spray can reduce how well it works. If you’re unsure, contact us and we’ll come back if needed.

When will I see results?

Weeds usually curl and die within 10 to 14 days. Grass gets thicker and greener over the first few treatments.
