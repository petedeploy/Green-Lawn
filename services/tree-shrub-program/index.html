Tree & Shrub Care questions

Hiring a lawn company for the first time can feel like a gamble. Here are straight answers to what homeowners ask us first.

Get a free estimate

or call 888-581-5296

What does the program include?

Seven seasonal visits with pest and disease treatments, deep-root fertilization and seasonal sprays.

Why use a professional?

Certified technicians spot pest, disease and weather stress early, before it turns into a costly replacement.

How often are treatments applied?

Seven visits spaced across the growing season, timed to each plant’s needs.

Which pests and diseases are covered?

Mites, aphids, scale, beetles and fungal diseases such as leaf spot and powdery mildew.

When is the best time to fertilize?

Spring and fall, with a late-fall feeding to strengthen roots before winter.

Does it include pruning?

No. We’ll recommend pruning separately if your plants need it.
