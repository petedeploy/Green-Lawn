Weeds that spread fast

Poor soil health

How much does lawn care cost?

Programs start at $54 per application, with 8 applications a year, based on a 3,000 sq ft lawn. We measure your lawn and confirm your exact price in your free estimate.

Is it safe for my kids and pets?

Yes, once the application dries. On a dry day that’s usually within a couple of hours.

Do I need to be home?

No. Your technician leaves a lawn flag and a service summary after every visit.

What if I’m not happy with the results?

Our lawn program comes with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you’re not happy, we’ll come back.

When will I see results?

Weeds usually curl and die within 10 to 14 days. Grass gets thicker and greener over the first few treatments.

Do I have to sign a contract?

Answer needed: contract and cancellation terms.
