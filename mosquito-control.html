Mosquito Control questions

How long does a treatment last?

About 3 to 4 weeks. After that, activity can return without another treatment.

How often should I treat?

Every 3 to 4 weeks from spring through early fall.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes, once dry. Keep children and pets off treated areas for about an hour.

Does it work?

Regular professional treatments noticeably reduce bites.

When should I start?

Around April, before summer populations peak.
