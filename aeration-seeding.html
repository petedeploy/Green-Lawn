Aeration & Seeding questions

Hiring a lawn company for the first time can feel like a gamble. Here are straight answers to what homeowners ask us first.

What is aeration, and why does my lawn need it?

Aeration pulls small plugs of soil to relieve compaction, so air, water and nutrients reach the roots.

When is the best time?

Late summer to early fall is ideal. Spring is a backup option.

How often should I aerate?

Most lawns need it once a year. Heavy soil or high-traffic areas can benefit from more.

How soon can I mow?

Wait two to three weeks.

When will I see new grass?

Usually within 1 to 3 weeks, depending on grass type and watering.

Will it damage sprinklers or an invisible dog fence?

Mark your sprinkler heads and tell the crew where the dog fence runs.

Should I rake up the soil plugs?

No. They break down on their own and feed the soil.
