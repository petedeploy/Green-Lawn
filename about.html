It started with one kid mowing lawns in Havertown.

Founder Matt Jesson cut his neighbors’ grass through his early twenties, then founded Green Lawn Fertilizing in 2004. Today we’re one of the largest independent lawn care companies in the Tri-State region, and still locally owned.
